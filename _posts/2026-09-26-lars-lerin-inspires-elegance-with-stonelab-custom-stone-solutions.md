Current trends in interior stone design celebrate both bold statements and understated luxury. Key directions include:

- **Monochromatic Palettes:** Inspired by the serene tones found in Scandinavian art and landscapes, neutral stones such as creamy marbles and matte-finished granites are favored for their calming influence.

- **Sustainable Choices:** Eco-conscious clients are seeking luxury interior materials that are responsibly sourced, reflecting a growing preference for both beauty and environmental stewardship.

## Selecting the Perfect Stone for Every Space

Choosing the right stone is essential for both function and aesthetic harmony. StoneLab guides clients through every step, ensuring each selection is perfectly attuned to the space and its intended use.

**For Kitchens:**  
Opt for durable, low-porosity stones for kitchen stone countertops. Materials like quartzite and granite combine resilience with visual appeal, standing up to daily use while maintaining their elegant finish.

**For Bathrooms:**  
Marble bathroom design remains a hallmark of luxury, but consider sealed limestone or engineered stones for high-traffic family spaces, ensuring both glamour and practicality.

**For Living Areas:**  
Bespoke stone furniture and feature walls in living spaces highlight unique veining and textures. These custom pieces become focal points, reflecting both personal style and the timeless appeal of natural stone.

## Care and Maintenance for Lasting Beauty

Maintaining the pristine look of stone surfaces is straightforward with the right approach:

- **Regular Cleaning:** Use a mild, pH-neutral cleaner and a soft cloth to preserve stone’s natural sheen.
- **Protective Sealing:** Annual sealing helps guard against stains and etching, particularly for marble and limestone.
- **Prompt Spill Management:** Quickly wipe up spills—especially acidic substances—to prevent discoloration.
- **Professional Restoration:** For treasured stonework, periodic professional care ensures surfaces remain as striking as the day they were installed.

## Choosing the Right Stone for Each Space in Your Scandinavian or Northern European Home

Selecting the ideal stone is both an art and a science. Here are some practical considerations:

- **Kitchen:** Opt for durable stones such as granite or quartzite for countertops. Their resilience to heat and scratches makes them ideal for busy family kitchens.
- **Bathroom:** Marble bathroom design remains a classic, offering a spa-like ambiance. For a modern twist, consider limestone or travertine, both of which evoke the natural charm of Scandinavian islands.
- **Living Areas:** Bespoke stone furniture or feature walls in limestone or sandstone can anchor a living room with subtle luxury.
- **Outdoor Spaces:** For terraces or patios, choose stones with weather-resistant properties, ensuring both beauty and longevity.

## Caring for Your Exclusive Stone Interiors to Maintain Luxury and Longevity

To maintain the brilliance of your interior stone design, follow these essential care tips:

- **Routine Cleaning:** Use a soft cloth and pH-neutral cleaner to avoid etching or dulling the surface.
- **Seal Regularly:** Many types of stone benefit from periodic sealing, which protects against stains and moisture.
- **Avoid Abrasives:** Refrain from using harsh chemicals or abrasive pads, as they can damage the stone’s finish.
- **Prompt Spill Cleanup:** Wipe spills immediately, especially acidic substances like wine or citrus, to prevent staining.

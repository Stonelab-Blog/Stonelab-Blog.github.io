## Choosing the Right Stone for Every Space in Germany, UK, and Beyond

Selecting the perfect stone begins with understanding each room’s function, light exposure, and desired atmosphere.

- **Kitchens:** Opt for durable granite or quartzite for kitchen stone countertops, balancing daily resilience with sophisticated style.
- **Bathrooms:** Calacatta marble or Swedish limestone elevates marble bathroom design, creating spa-like sanctuaries.
- **Living Areas:** Bespoke stone furniture and mantels introduce tactile interest and visual harmony.

Collaborate with experts to source exclusive stone interiors that reflect your aesthetic and lifestyle requirements.

## Care and Maintenance Tips for Lasting Beauty

To preserve the allure of custom stone installations:

- **Regular Cleaning:** Use pH-neutral cleaners and avoid abrasive tools.
- **Seal Surfaces:** Periodically reseal to protect against stains, especially in high-traffic or wet areas.
- **Prompt Spill Management:** Wipe spills immediately to prevent etching or discoloration.
- **Professional Assessment:** Schedule routine inspections to ensure the integrity of seams and finishes.

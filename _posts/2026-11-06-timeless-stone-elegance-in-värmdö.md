Modern aesthetics in Northern Europe—including Sweden, Germany, and the UK—increasingly favour clean lines, tactile surfaces, and natural hues. Here are some of the most notable trends shaping exclusive stone interiors:

- **Integrated Stone Details:** Subtle stone accents in window sills, shelving, and even lighting fixtures create a cohesive, luxury interior.

## Selecting the Right Stone for Every Space in Northern Europe

Choosing the best stone for your project requires a balance of style, function, and sustainability. Here’s a guide to help you make informed decisions:

- **Kitchens:** Opt for hardwearing stones like quartzite or granite for countertops and islands. These materials resist stains and scratches, making them ideal for busy culinary spaces.
- **Bathrooms:** Marble’s natural veining and cool touch make it a popular choice for vanities and shower walls. For added slip resistance, consider a honed or brushed finish.
- **Living Areas:** Limestone and travertine bring warmth and texture to floors and feature walls, while bespoke stone furniture adds bespoke sophistication.

## Caring for Your Stone Surfaces in Luxury Interiors

To maintain the pristine beauty of your stone interiors:

- **Clean regularly** with a pH-neutral cleaner and a soft cloth.
- **Seal surfaces** periodically, especially in high-traffic or wet areas, to protect against stains.
- **Avoid harsh chemicals** that can etch or dull natural stone.
- **Address spills promptly** to prevent long-term damage, particularly on marble or limestone.

### Choosing the Right Stone for Every Space

Selecting the ideal stone for your interior begins with understanding its unique properties and how they complement your lifestyle and aesthetic vision.

#### For Kitchens

Opt for hardwearing surfaces such as granite or engineered stone for kitchen countertops. These materials offer resilience against stains and scratches, ensuring longevity without compromising on elegance.

#### For Bathrooms

Marble bathroom design remains a hallmark of luxury. Its smooth, luminous finish provides a serene atmosphere, perfect for relaxation. Consider honed or matte finishes for added slip resistance and a contemporary touch.

#### For Living Areas

Bespoke stone furniture, including custom coffee tables and feature walls, brings natural artistry into social spaces. Neutral tones in travertine or limestone harmonize with Scandinavian interiors, while bold patterns in onyx or quartzite create striking focal points.

### Caring for Your Stone Surfaces

Preserving the beauty of exclusive stone interiors requires thoughtful maintenance:

- **Routine Cleaning**: Use pH-neutral cleaners and soft cloths to prevent surface etching.
- **Sealing**: Periodically seal porous stones like marble and limestone to protect against moisture and stains.
- **Immediate Attention**: Wipe spills promptly, especially acidic substances, to maintain the stone’s pristine finish.
- **Protective Measures**: Use coasters and trivets on kitchen stone countertops and tables to avoid scratches and heat damage.

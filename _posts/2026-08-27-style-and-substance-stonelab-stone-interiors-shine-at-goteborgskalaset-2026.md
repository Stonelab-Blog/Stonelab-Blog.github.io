## Selecting the Right Stone for Every Space in Scandinavian Luxury Interiors

Choosing the ideal stone for your project requires consideration of both aesthetics and performance. Here are practical guidelines for selecting premium stonework tailored to luxury homeowners and design professionals in Scandinavia and Northern Europe:

### Kitchens

- **Material:** Opt for hardwearing surfaces like granite or quartzite for kitchen stone countertops, ensuring resistance to stains and scratches.
- **Finish:** A honed or leathered finish provides a sophisticated matte look while concealing daily wear.

### Bathrooms

- **Material:** Marble remains the top choice for a serene, spa-like bathroom. Its natural veining brings a sense of luxury and individuality.
- **Care:** Seal marble regularly to protect against moisture, and use pH-neutral cleaners to preserve its polish.

### Living Spaces

- **Feature Walls & Furniture:** Bespoke stone furniture and accent pieces in travertine or limestone create subtle elegance and can be tailored to suit minimalist or maximalist schemes.

## Stone Care and Maintenance: Ensuring Lasting Beauty in Exclusive Stone Interiors

To keep your exclusive stone interiors pristine:

- **Clean Spills Promptly:** Especially on marble bathroom design and kitchen surfaces, prevent stains by wiping spills immediately.
- **Use Gentle Products:** Avoid acidic or abrasive cleaners. Choose soft cloths and pH-neutral solutions.
- **Seal Regularly:** Stone surfaces benefit from routine sealing to maintain their lustre and repel moisture.
- **Protect Surfaces:** Use trivets, coasters, and mats on kitchen countertops and bespoke stone furniture.

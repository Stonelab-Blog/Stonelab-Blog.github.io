## Selecting the Right Stone for Your Space in Scandinavia and Northern Europe

Choosing the ideal stone is both an art and a science. Here’s how to make informed choices for different areas:

### Kitchens

Opt for durable stones like granite or quartzite for countertops, ensuring resistance to heat and scratches. Consider light-toned stones for a spacious, airy feel, or dramatic, veined marble for a statement island.

### Bathrooms

Marble and limestone are favorites for their luxurious appeal. Choose honed finishes for slip resistance and a modern look. Large slabs minimize grout lines for easy maintenance and a seamless aesthetic.

### Living Spaces

Bespoke stone furniture—such as coffee tables or shelving—adds an exclusive touch. Select unique stones with distinctive patterns to create a conversation piece.

## Care and Maintenance Tips for Luxury Stone Interiors

To preserve the beauty of your exclusive stone interiors:

- **Seal surfaces** regularly to prevent staining.
- **Clean with pH-neutral products** to avoid etching or dullness.
- **Wipe spills immediately**, especially on marble, to protect against acids.
- **Avoid abrasive pads** which can scratch the finish.

With the right care, your custom stone solutions will retain their luster for generations.

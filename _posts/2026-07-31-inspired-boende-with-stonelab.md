## Choosing the Right Stone for Every Space

Selecting the ideal stone requires balancing aesthetic vision with practical needs. Here are some expert tips:

### Kitchen

- **Recommended:** Durable granite or quartzite for kitchen stone countertops.
- **Why:** These stones resist scratching, staining, and heat, ideal for busy households.
- **Trend:** Waterfall edges and integrated sinks for seamless luxury.

### Bathroom

- **Recommended:** Classic marble or contemporary terrazzo for marble bathroom design.
- **Why:** Marble offers timeless beauty, while terrazzo provides playful color and pattern.
- **Trend:** Large-format slabs for minimal seams and easy maintenance.

### Living Spaces

- **Recommended:** Limestone, travertine, or bespoke stone furniture pieces.
- **Why:** Softer stones suit low-traffic areas and offer tactile warmth.
- **Trend:** Statement coffee tables or shelving that highlight unique veining.

## Caring for Your Stone Surfaces

StoneLab’s exclusive stone interiors are crafted to stand the test of time. To keep surfaces pristine:

- **Clean regularly** with a soft cloth and pH-neutral cleaner.
- **Seal porous stones** (like marble and limestone) annually to prevent stains.
- **Use coasters and trivets** to protect from heat and moisture.
- **Avoid harsh chemicals** and abrasive sponges.

With these simple routines, your stone features will remain as stunning as the day they were installed.

## Selecting the Right Stone for Every Space

Choosing the perfect stone requires an understanding of both aesthetic goals and practical considerations. Here are a few expert tips:

- **Kitchens:** Opt for resilient stones such as granite or engineered quartz for countertops—these materials withstand heavy use while showcasing refined beauty.
- **Bathrooms:** Marble and limestone create a serene, spa-like atmosphere, but require sealants to protect against moisture and staining.
- **Living Spaces:** For bespoke stone furniture and accent walls, consider onyx, travertine, or sandstone to introduce warmth and tactile interest.
- **Outdoor Areas:** Weather-resistant stones like slate and basalt are ideal for terraces and garden features.

## Care and Maintenance for Lasting Beauty

Stone surfaces are an investment in lasting luxury. Preserve their elegance with these essential care practices:

- **Routine Cleaning:** Use a soft cloth and pH-neutral cleaner to maintain the surface’s natural luster.
- **Protection:** Apply sealants to porous stones regularly, especially in kitchens and bathrooms.
- **Avoid Abrasives:** Refrain from using harsh chemicals or rough sponges, which can damage the finish.
- **Prompt Spill Management:** Quickly wipe away liquids to prevent staining, particularly with marble and limestone.

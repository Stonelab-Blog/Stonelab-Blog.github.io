## Practical Advice for Selecting the Right Stone

Choosing the perfect stone requires balancing aesthetics, function, and maintenance. Here are a few expert tips:

- **Consider the Space:** For high-traffic areas like kitchens, opt for durable stones such as granite or engineered quartz. In bathrooms, marble remains a classic for its refined look.
- **Match Color and Texture:** Select hues and finishes that complement your overall design palette. Soft, matte finishes are trending in Scandinavian-inspired interiors.
- **Think Beyond Surfaces:** Explore options like bespoke stone furniture or accent walls to add a unique touch.

## Care and Maintenance Tips

Luxury interior materials deserve attentive care. Follow these guidelines to preserve the beauty of your custom stone features:

- **Clean Gently:** Use pH-neutral cleaners and a soft cloth for daily maintenance.
- **Seal Regularly:** Protect porous stones like marble by resealing surfaces as recommended.
- **Prevent Damage:** Avoid harsh chemicals and use trivets or mats under hot cookware.

### What makes StoneLab's custom stone solutions unique in Scandinavian interior design?

Our bespoke stone furniture, kitchen stone countertops, and marble bathroom designs are tailored to fit luxury interiors across Sweden and Northern Europe, emphasizing sustainability and exclusive design.

### How does StoneLab ensure sustainable stone sourcing?

We prioritize partnerships with responsible quarries and local suppliers, minimizing environmental impact while maintaining the highest quality standards. Sustainable stone sourcing is integral to our process, aligning with green practices favored by luxury homeowners and designers.

### Which types of stone are best suited for kitchen countertops and bathroom designs?

For kitchen stone countertops, durable materials like granite and engineered quartz are ideal due to their resistance to wear and heat. Marble remains a preferred choice for bathroom designs because of its timeless elegance and refined appearance, though it requires proper sealing and care.

### Can StoneLab create bespoke stone furniture for luxury interiors?

Yes, StoneLab offers bespoke stone furniture crafted to complement exclusive interiors. Our custom designs include sculptural pieces that serve both functional and artistic purposes, enhancing the uniqueness of your space.

### How can I maintain and care for my stone interiors from StoneLab?

To maintain your luxury stone interiors, clean surfaces gently with pH-neutral products, reseal porous stones such as marble regularly, and avoid harsh chemicals. Using protective mats or trivets under hot cookware also helps preserve the stone’s finish and longevity.

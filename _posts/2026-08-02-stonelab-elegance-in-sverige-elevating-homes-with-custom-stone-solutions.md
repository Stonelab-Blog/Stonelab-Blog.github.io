## How to Select the Right Stone for Every Space

Choosing the perfect stone is both an art and a science. Here are key considerations for different areas of your home:

### Kitchen Stone Countertops

- **Durability:** Granite and quartzite are excellent choices for high-traffic kitchens, offering resilience and ease of maintenance.
- **Aesthetics:** Marble remains a favourite for its luminous appeal, especially when paired with minimalist cabinetry and hardware.

### Marble Bathroom Design

- **Water Resistance:** Select non-porous stones such as marble or engineered quartz for bathroom vanities and shower walls.
- **Visual Impact:** Large-format slabs reduce seams and create a spa-like ambiance, especially when illuminated with soft, indirect lighting.

### Bespoke Stone Furniture

- **Customization:** Work with StoneLab’s artisans to design unique pieces that complement your interior—think sculpted coffee tables or statement dining surfaces.
- **Functionality:** Consider the intended use, as softer stones may be better suited for decorative furniture, while harder stones excel in daily-use items.

## Maintaining the Beauty of Your Exclusive Stone Interiors

StoneLab’s interior stone design is meant to last a lifetime, but a few simple practices ensure its enduring beauty:

- **Clean Regularly:** Use a mild, pH-neutral cleaner and a soft cloth. Avoid acidic or abrasive products.
- **Seal Surfaces:** Many natural stones benefit from periodic sealing to protect against stains and moisture.
- **Prevent Scratches:** Use coasters and trivets on kitchen stone countertops and avoid dragging heavy items across stone surfaces.

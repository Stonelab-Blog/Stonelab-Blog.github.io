## Practical Guidance for Selecting Stone

Selecting the right stone for your space involves careful consideration of both form and function:

- **For Kitchens:** Opt for robust materials like granite or quartzite for kitchen stone countertops. These stones resist stains and scratches while providing a luxurious finish.
- **For Bathrooms:** Marble bathroom design remains a hallmark of luxury. Choose honed or polished finishes to enhance the sense of calm and opulence.
- **For Living Areas:** Statement fireplace surrounds and bespoke stone furniture pieces add drama and elegance to social spaces.

### Tips for Stone Care and Maintenance

- **Sealing:** Regularly seal porous stones, such as marble or limestone, to prevent staining.
- **Cleaning:** Use pH-neutral cleaners to avoid etching or dulling the stone’s surface.
- **Daily Care:** Wipe spills immediately and use coasters or trivets to protect surfaces from heat and moisture.

## Selecting the Right Stone for Each Space

Choosing the perfect stone requires balancing visual appeal with practical considerations. Here’s how to approach each space:

- **Kitchen:** Opt for durable materials like granite or engineered quartz for kitchen stone countertops. They offer resistance to heat and scratches, perfect for busy culinary environments.
- **Bathroom:** Marble bathroom design remains a classic for its luxurious feel, but consider honed finishes for slip resistance and easier maintenance.
- **Living Areas:** Limestone or travertine adds warmth and timeless elegance to living rooms or entryways. For bespoke stone furniture, select stones with unique veining to create a bespoke focal point.

## Care and Maintenance Tips

To preserve the beauty of exclusive stone interiors:

- **Seal Surfaces:** Regularly apply sealant to protect against stains and moisture.
- **Gentle Cleaning:** Use pH-neutral cleaners and avoid harsh chemicals to maintain the stone’s natural sheen.
- **Quick Responses:** Wipe spills immediately to prevent etching or staining, especially on marble and limestone.
- **Professional Advice:** Consult with experts like StoneLab for tailored maintenance routines suited to each stone type.

## Selecting the Right Stone for Every Space

Choosing the ideal stone is both an art and a science. Here are some tips to guide your selection:

- **Kitchens:** For kitchen stone countertops, durability is paramount. Granite and quartzite offer exceptional resistance to heat and scratches, while marble brings unmatched sophistication for less demanding surfaces.
- **Bathrooms:** Marble bathroom design exudes calm and refinement. Choose stones with low porosity to withstand humidity and daily use.
- **Living Spaces:** Bespoke stone furniture or feature walls in living areas create dramatic focal points. Limestone or travertine offer subtle textures and warm tones.
- **Sustainability:** Opt for stones sourced from quarries with responsible practices, reducing transport emissions and environmental impact.

## Caring for Your Stone Surfaces

The beauty of exclusive stone interiors is matched by their longevity—provided they are cared for correctly. To keep your investment looking its best:

- **Regular Maintenance:** Wipe surfaces with a soft, damp cloth and pH-neutral cleaner.
- **Sealant Application:** Most natural stones benefit from periodic sealing to prevent staining and moisture absorption.
- **Avoid Harsh Chemicals:** Acidic or abrasive cleaners can damage the surface finish.
- **Prompt Spill Response:** Clean up spills quickly, especially on more porous stones like marble.

## Choosing the Right Stone for Every Space

Selecting stone is both an art and a science. Kajsa Fredholm and the StoneLab team guide clients through every decision, ensuring each choice reflects both aesthetic aspirations and practical needs.

**For Kitchens:**  
Durability and style are paramount. Granite and engineered quartz are popular for kitchen stone countertops, offering resilience to heat and wear while complementing sleek Scandinavian designs.

**For Bathrooms:**  
Marble bathroom design remains a classic choice, infusing spaces with calm luxury. Consider moisture resistance and slip-proof finishes for added safety and longevity.

**For Living Spaces and Furniture:**  
Bespoke stone furniture in travertine or limestone creates striking focal points, with each piece carefully crafted to fit its environment.

### Tips for Stone Selection

- **Assess the room’s function:** High-traffic areas require robust stones like granite or quartzite.
- **Match tones with lighting:** Natural light can enhance or tone down the stone’s hues—always view samples on site.
- **Think long-term:** Choose timeless colors and patterns for enduring appeal.

## Keeping Stone Surfaces Pristine

Premium stone deserves attentive care. Here are essential maintenance tips:

- **Routine Cleaning:** Use a soft cloth and pH-neutral cleaner. Avoid acidic products that can etch surfaces.
- **Sealing:** Most natural stones benefit from periodic sealing to repel stains and moisture.
- **Immediate Attention:** Wipe spills promptly, especially liquids like wine or citrus that can stain or damage.

## Selecting the Perfect Stone for Each Space in Luxury Homes

Choosing the right stone is crucial for both aesthetics and practicality. Here’s how to approach selection for various rooms common among luxury homeowners and designers in Northern Europe:

### Kitchen

- **Best Choices:** Durable granites and quartzites for kitchen stone countertops.
- **Why:** These stones resist staining and scratching, ideal for high-traffic culinary spaces.
- **Tip:** Opt for lighter shades to enhance natural light and make the kitchen feel more spacious.

### Bathroom

- **Best Choices:** Marble for a spa-like marble bathroom design, or limestone for subtle elegance.
- **Why:** Both offer water resistance and timeless beauty.
- **Tip:** Ensure surfaces are properly sealed to maintain their luxurious appearance.

### Living Spaces

- **Best Choices:** Statement fireplace surrounds, bespoke stone furniture, and accent walls.
- **Why:** These elements become focal points, adding gravitas and personality to interiors.
- **Tip:** Mix and match textures—polished, honed, and fluted—for visual depth.

## Care and Maintenance Tips for Exclusive Stone Interiors

Stone surfaces are an investment. To ensure lasting beauty:

- **Daily Care:** Wipe surfaces with a damp, soft cloth and pH-neutral cleaner.
- **Sealing:** Reseal marble and limestone annually to protect from stains and moisture.
- **Avoid:** Harsh chemicals, acidic cleaners, and abrasive pads that may damage the stone’s finish.

For marble bathroom designs, regular cleaning with pH-neutral cleaners and annual sealing is essential to maintain the stone’s beauty and durability. Avoid acidic or abrasive cleaners to prevent damage to the marble’s surface.

## Selecting the Perfect Stone for Every Room

Choosing the right stone is essential for both aesthetics and functionality. Here are expert tips from StoneLab’s interior stone design specialists:

- **Kitchens:** Opt for durable, low-maintenance materials like granite or quartzite for stone countertops. For a statement island, consider richly veined marble.
- **Bathrooms:** Marble offers enduring luxury, while limestone and travertine provide a softer, spa-like ambiance. Ensure surfaces are sealed properly to prevent staining.
- **Living Areas:** Bespoke stone furniture—coffee tables, consoles, or feature walls—adds sophistication and lasting value to living spaces.
- **Entryways:** Hard-wearing stones such as granite or terrazzo create an inviting yet resilient entrance.

## Caring for Your Stone Surfaces

To preserve the beauty of your luxury interior materials:

- Wipe spills promptly, especially on marble bathroom design elements.
- Use gentle, pH-neutral cleaners and avoid abrasive pads.
- Apply sealants regularly, as recommended by StoneLab’s team.
- Place protective mats under hot objects on kitchen stone countertops to prevent thermal shock.

### What types of custom stone solutions does StoneLab offer for luxury interiors in Malmö?

### How does StoneLab ensure sustainable stone sourcing for its projects?

StoneLab partners with ethical quarries and prioritizes local materials to reduce environmental impact. Their sustainable stone sourcing approach combines responsible procurement with innovative technology to deliver eco-conscious luxury stone interiors.

### Can StoneLab assist architects and interior designers with exclusive stone interiors in Northern Europe?

Yes, StoneLab collaborates closely with architects, interior designers, and developers across Sweden, Scandinavia, Northern Europe, Germany, and the UK to create exclusive stone interiors that reflect Swedish stone craftsmanship and bespoke design.

### What are the best stone materials recommended by StoneLab for kitchen countertops and bathrooms?

For kitchens, StoneLab recommends durable and low-maintenance stones like granite and quartzite, while richly veined marble is ideal for statement islands. For bathrooms, marble, limestone, and travertine are preferred for their luxurious and spa-like qualities.

### How should luxury homeowners maintain their stone surfaces to preserve quality?

StoneLab advises prompt cleaning of spills, especially on marble surfaces, using gentle, pH-neutral cleaners, avoiding abrasive materials, applying sealants regularly, and protecting kitchen stone countertops from thermal shock with mats under hot objects.

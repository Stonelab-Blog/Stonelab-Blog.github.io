## Current Trends in Exclusive Stone Interiors

In recent years, we are witnessing a renaissance in interior stone design across Scandinavia and beyond. Noteworthy style trends include:

- **Minimalist Luxury:** Clean lines, seamless surfaces, and understated color palettes highlight the organic beauty of materials like marble and granite.
- **Sustainable Stone Sourcing:** Eco-conscious clients are seeking responsibly sourced stone, prioritizing ethical extraction and reduced environmental impact.
- **Textured Finishes:** Fluted details, matte surfaces, and honed finishes bring tactile interest to walls, floors, and furniture.
- **Statement Bathrooms:** Marble bathroom design featuring bold veining and integrated stone sinks is highly sought after.
- **Integrated Stone Furniture:** Custom tables, shelving, and hearths crafted from natural stone provide functional artistry.

## Choosing the Right Stone for Every Space

Selecting the perfect stone is both an aesthetic and practical decision. Here are a few expert tips from StoneLab:

### Kitchens

- **Recommended Stones:** Quartzite, granite, and engineered stone excel as kitchen stone countertops for their durability and stain resistance.
- **Advice:** Opt for lighter shades in smaller kitchens to enhance light and create an open atmosphere.

### Bathrooms

- **Recommended Stones:** Marble and limestone offer spa-like elegance for vanities, walls, and floors.
- **Advice:** Consider slip-resistant finishes for safety, and select stones with natural water resistance.

### Living Areas & Furniture

- **Recommended Stones:** Travertine, granite, and onyx create striking bespoke stone furniture and statement fireplaces.
- **Advice:** Choose tones that harmonize with your interior palette; textured stones add depth and visual interest.

## Caring for Your Stone Surfaces

To preserve the beauty of your exclusive stone interiors, regular care is essential:

- Use pH-neutral cleaners designed for natural stone.
- Wipe spills promptly, especially for porous stones like marble.
- Apply a professional sealant periodically to protect against stains and moisture.
- Avoid abrasive pads or harsh chemicals that may damage the stone’s finish.

### Which types of stone are best for luxury kitchen countertops?

Quartzite, granite, and engineered stone are recommended for kitchen stone countertops due to their durability, stain resistance, and timeless appeal. StoneLab advises selecting lighter shades in smaller kitchens to optimize light and space.

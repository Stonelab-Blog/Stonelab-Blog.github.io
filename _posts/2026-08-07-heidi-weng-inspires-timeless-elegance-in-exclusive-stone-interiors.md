## How to Choose the Perfect Stone for Every Luxury Interior Stone Design Project

Selecting the right stone is as much about lifestyle as it is about aesthetics. Here are StoneLab’s expert recommendations for clients in Northern Europe and the UK:

- **Kitchens:** Opt for durable surfaces such as granite or high-quality quartzite for kitchen stone countertops. These materials withstand daily use while offering a refined look.
- **Bathrooms:** Marble bathroom design remains a classic, but consider non-porous stones like engineered quartz for added practicality and ease of maintenance.
- **Living Areas & Furniture:** Limestone and travertine provide a soft, inviting palette ideal for bespoke stone furniture and statement pieces.
- **Accent Walls & Fireplaces:** Dramatic stones like onyx or richly veined marbles create unforgettable focal points in luxury interior materials.

## Caring for Your Stone Surfaces

To preserve the flawless finish of your exclusive stone interiors crafted by StoneLab:

- **Clean regularly** with a mild, pH-neutral cleaner and a soft cloth.
- **Seal porous stones** annually to protect against staining and etching.
- **Avoid harsh chemicals** or abrasive pads that may dull the surface.
- **Wipe spills immediately,** especially on marble and limestone, to maintain their pristine appearance.

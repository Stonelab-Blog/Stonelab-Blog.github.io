## Selecting the Perfect Stone for Every Space

Choosing the right stone is essential for achieving both visual harmony and lasting performance. Here are StoneLab’s expert recommendations for key areas:

- **Kitchens:** Opt for durable granite or quartzite for kitchen stone countertops to withstand daily use while exuding elegance.
- **Bathrooms:** Marble bathroom design remains a favorite for its luxuriously smooth finish and unique veining.
- **Living Spaces:** Limestone and travertine offer understated sophistication, ideal for accent walls or bespoke stone furniture.
- **Outdoor Areas:** Frost-resistant stones like granite ensure longevity and beauty in Scandinavian climates.

### Practical Care and Maintenance Tips

A luxury stone surface deserves attentive care. Here’s how to keep your investment at its best:

- **Seal Regularly:** Most natural stones benefit from annual sealing to repel stains and moisture.
- **Gentle Cleaning:** Use pH-neutral cleaners and avoid harsh chemicals to protect the finish.
- **Immediate Spill Management:** Wipe up spills promptly, especially on marble, to prevent etching.

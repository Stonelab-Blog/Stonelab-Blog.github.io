## Selecting the Right Stone for Every Space

Choosing the ideal stone for your space is both an art and a science. Here’s how to make inspired choices:

- **Kitchens:** Opt for durable, low-porosity stones such as quartzite or granite for countertops that endure daily use and still look magnificent.
- **Bathrooms:** Marble remains a favorite for its soft veining and luxurious feel, perfect for serene spa-like retreats.
- **Living Areas:** Dramatic feature walls or fireplace surrounds in textured limestone or travertine lend warmth and personality.
- **Furniture:** Bespoke pieces in onyx or high-contrast marble can transform a room into a gallery.

*Tip: Always consider the intended use, maintenance requirements, and natural lighting of the space when selecting your stone.*

## Care and Maintenance for Lasting Beauty

To preserve the natural allure of your exclusive stone interiors:

- **Seal surfaces** regularly to prevent staining and moisture absorption.
- **Use pH-neutral cleaners** specifically formulated for stone.
- **Wipe spills promptly**—especially acidic substances like wine or citrus—to protect the finish.
- **Avoid harsh abrasives** that can scratch or dull the surface.

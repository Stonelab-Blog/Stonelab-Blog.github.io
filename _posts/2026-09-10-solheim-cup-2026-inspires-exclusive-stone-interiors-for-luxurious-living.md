## How to Select the Perfect Stone for Every Space

Choosing the right stone is an art. Here’s how to ensure each material complements your vision and lifestyle:

### Kitchen Stone Countertops
- **Consider durability:** Granite and quartzite resist stains and scratches, ideal for high-traffic kitchens.
- **Think about maintenance:** Lighter marbles are stunning but may require more frequent sealing.
- **Match the aesthetic:** Bold veining or subtle patterns can transform the visual energy of your kitchen.

### Marble Bathroom Design
- **Prioritize water resistance:** Proper sealing safeguards against moisture and maintains brilliance.
- **Select for ambiance:** Cool-toned marbles evoke spa-like serenity, while darker stones create dramatic intimacy.

### Bespoke Stone Furniture
- **Choose statement pieces:** A custom stone table or bench becomes the centerpiece of any room.
- **Coordinate finishes:** Harmonize with existing luxury interior materials for a seamless look.

## Care and Maintenance Tips for Lasting Beauty

StoneLab’s exclusive stone interiors are designed to last. Maintain their elegance with these simple steps:

- **Clean gently:** Use pH-neutral cleaners and soft cloths to avoid dulling the surface.
- **Seal regularly:** Most natural stones benefit from periodic sealing to resist stains.
- **Protect from heat and spills:** Use trivets and wipe up spills promptly, especially on marble and limestone.

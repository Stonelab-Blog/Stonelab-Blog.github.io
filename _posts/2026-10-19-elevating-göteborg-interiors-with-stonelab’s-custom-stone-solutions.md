### Choosing the Right Stone for Your Space

Selecting the perfect stone is both an art and a science. Here are practical tips from StoneLab’s experts:

- **Kitchens:** Opt for durable, low-porosity materials like granite or engineered stone for kitchen stone countertops. These resist stains and scratches, making them ideal for high-traffic areas.  
- **Bathrooms:** Embrace the luxury of marble bathroom design, but ensure proper sealing and ventilation to maintain its pristine allure.  
- **Living Areas:** Consider limestone or travertine for softer, warmer hues in lounge and dining spaces. For statement pieces, bespoke stone furniture in unique finishes can anchor the room’s aesthetic.  
- **Outdoor Spaces:** Choose frost-resistant stones like slate or granite for patios and terraces, ensuring longevity in the Scandinavian climate.  

### Caring for Your Stone Surfaces

To preserve the elegance and performance of your custom stone solutions, follow these essential care tips:

- **Regular cleaning** with pH-neutral cleansers to avoid etching or dulling the surface.  
- **Promptly wipe spills**—especially acidic substances like wine or citrus—to prevent staining on softer stones.  
- **Annual sealing** for porous stones such as marble and limestone to maintain resistance against moisture and stains.  
- **Use coasters and mats** to protect stone furniture and countertops from scratches and heat.

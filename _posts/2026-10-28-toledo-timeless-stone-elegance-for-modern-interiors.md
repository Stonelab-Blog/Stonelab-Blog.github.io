### Current Style Trends: The Toledo Influence

The “Toledo” trend is characterized by arched forms, warm neutral palettes, and a harmonious mix of textures. Softened marble, travertine, and limestone surfaces bring a sense of calm to kitchens and living areas, while dramatic, custom-carved stonework becomes a focal point in entryways or spa-inspired bathrooms.

**Trending applications for 2024:**
- **Kitchen Stone Countertops:** Honed finishes and waterfall edges are redefining the modern kitchen, blending practicality with sculptural beauty.
- **Marble Bathroom Design:** Large-format stone slabs and integrated sinks create seamless, spa-like atmospheres.
- **Bespoke Stone Furniture:** Statement tables, benches, and shelving in natural stone anchor open-plan living spaces with substance and grace.

### Selecting the Right Stone for Each Space

Choosing the ideal stone depends on both functional needs and aesthetic preferences. Here are a few tips for luxury homeowners, architects, and interior designers:

- **Kitchens:** Opt for durable stones like granite or engineered quartz for countertops—these resist stains and scratches while maintaining a refined look.
- **Bathrooms:** Marble remains a top choice for its elegance, but consider sealed limestone or travertine for a softer, more organic feel.
- **Living Areas:** Statement walls or fireplaces in textured stone offer architectural interest and a tactile experience.
- **Furniture:** Bespoke stone pieces should complement the room’s palette and provide both beauty and practicality.

### Care and Maintenance for Lasting Beauty

Preserving the splendor of your interior stone design is simple with a few mindful habits:

- Use pH-neutral cleaners designed for natural stone.
- Wipe up spills immediately to prevent staining.
- Reseal surfaces periodically, especially in kitchens and bathrooms.
- Avoid abrasive pads when cleaning to maintain the finish.

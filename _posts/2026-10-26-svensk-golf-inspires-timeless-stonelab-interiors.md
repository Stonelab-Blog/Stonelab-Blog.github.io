## Choosing the Perfect Stone for Every Space

Selecting the right stone is crucial for both aesthetics and function. Here are tailored recommendations from StoneLab’s custom stone solutions:

- **Kitchens:** Opt for durable, non-porous stones like quartzite or select granites for kitchen stone countertops. These materials resist staining and scratching while exuding luxury.
- **Bathrooms:** Marble remains a classic for marble bathroom design, offering a luminous finish. For a modern twist, consider unique Swedish stones with subtle veining.
- **Living Areas:** Bespoke stone furniture such as coffee tables, shelving, or fireplace surrounds brings organic warmth and sophistication.
- **Entryways:** Statement flooring in natural stone sets the tone for exclusive stone interiors and withstands daily wear.

## Sustainable Stone Sourcing and Care

StoneLab is dedicated to sustainable stone sourcing, prioritizing materials from environmentally responsible quarries and favoring local Swedish suppliers. This minimizes carbon impact and ensures every piece meets the highest standards of Swedish stone craftsmanship.

To keep your stone surfaces looking pristine:

- **Clean regularly** with pH-neutral, non-abrasive cleaners.
- **Seal surfaces** periodically as recommended for your stone type.
- **Wipe spills promptly**—especially acidic substances on marble—to prevent etching or stains.
- **Use coasters and mats** under glasses and hot dishes on kitchen stone countertops.

Proper care preserves both the beauty and longevity of your luxury stone investments.

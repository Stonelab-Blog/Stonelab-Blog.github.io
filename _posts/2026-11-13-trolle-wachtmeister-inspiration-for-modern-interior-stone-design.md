### Choosing the Right Stone for Every Space

Selecting the perfect stone is both an art and a science. Here’s how to ensure your choice suits your vision and lifestyle:

#### For Kitchens
- **Granite** and **quartzite** are ideal for kitchen stone countertops, offering resilience against heat and stains.
- Opt for honed finishes for a contemporary, low-sheen look.

#### For Bathrooms
- **Marble** creates a luxurious spa ambiance, especially in shower surrounds and vanities.
- Ensure proper sealing to protect against moisture and preserve the stone's natural beauty.

#### For Living Areas and Furniture
- **Limestone** and **travertine** bring warmth and tactile interest to bespoke stone furniture and accent walls.
- Consider custom inlays or mixed materials for a truly exclusive statement.

### Care and Maintenance Tips for Lasting Beauty

Maintaining the allure of your stone interiors is simple with the right approach:

- Use pH-neutral cleaners specifically designed for natural stone.
- Wipe spills promptly to prevent staining.
- Reseal surfaces annually, especially in kitchens and bathrooms.
- Avoid abrasive pads or harsh chemicals that may damage the stone’s surface.

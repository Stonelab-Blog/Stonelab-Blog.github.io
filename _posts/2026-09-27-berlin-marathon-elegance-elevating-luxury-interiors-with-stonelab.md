### Choosing the Perfect Stone for Every Space

Selecting the right stone is essential for achieving both beauty and longevity. Consider these tips:

- **Kitchens:** Opt for robust materials like granite or quartzite for kitchen stone countertops. These withstand daily use while offering a range of sophisticated finishes.
- **Bathrooms:** Marble bathroom design remains a hallmark of luxury. Choose honed or matte finishes for a contemporary feel, and consider underfloor heating compatibility for added comfort.
- **Furniture and Accents:** Bespoke stone furniture, such as coffee tables or statement shelving, brings unique character to any room. StoneLab’s expertise ensures perfect harmony between form and function.

### Caring for Your Stone Surfaces

Maintain the allure of your exclusive stone interiors with these simple care guidelines:

- **Daily Cleaning:** Use a soft cloth and pH-neutral cleanser to avoid dulling finishes.
- **Sealing:** Periodically reseal surfaces like kitchen countertops or bathroom vanities to protect against stains and moisture.
- **Prompt Attention:** Wipe spills quickly, particularly acidic substances, to prevent etching or discoloration.

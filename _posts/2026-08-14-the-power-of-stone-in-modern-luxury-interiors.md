## Choosing the Right Stone for Every Luxury Interior Space

Selecting the ideal stone for your project is both an aesthetic and practical decision. Here’s how to approach it:

- **Kitchens:** Opt for durable stones like granite or engineered quartz for kitchen stone countertops, blending resilience with refined beauty.
- **Bathrooms:** Marble and limestone bring warmth and sophistication to marble bathroom design. Consider finishes that are easy to maintain and slip-resistant.
- **Living Areas and Furniture:** For bespoke stone furniture or feature walls, explore unique stones with dramatic patterning or subdued tones, depending on the desired ambiance.
- **Entryways:** Hardwearing stones like slate or basalt withstand heavy foot traffic while making a grand first impression.

## Stone Care and Maintenance Tips for Sustainable Stone Interiors

To preserve the power and beauty of your stone surfaces:

- **Seal and protect:** Regular sealing helps maintain the finish and resist stains.
- **Gentle cleaning:** Use pH-neutral cleaners and soft cloths for daily care.
- **Avoid harsh chemicals:** Steer clear of acidic products that can etch or dull the stone.
- **Immediate attention:** Wipe up spills promptly to prevent staining, especially on marble and limestone.

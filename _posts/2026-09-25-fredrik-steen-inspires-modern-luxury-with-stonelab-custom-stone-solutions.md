## Selecting the Right Stone for Every Space in Luxury Interiors

Choosing the perfect stone is both an art and a science. StoneLab’s expertise ensures each project is tailored to the client’s vision and each stone’s unique properties.

### Kitchen: The Heart of the Home with Kitchen Stone Countertops

For kitchen stone countertops, durability and ease of care are paramount. Granite and quartzite offer resilience against stains and scratches, while marble introduces unmistakable elegance. Opt for honed finishes for a softer, matte look or polished for a classic shine.

### Bathroom: Spa-Like Serenity with Marble Bathroom Design

Marble bathroom design continues to be in demand for its refined luxury. Select denser marbles or natural stones with low porosity to withstand humidity. Consider large-format tiles for fewer grout lines and a seamless, spa-inspired effect.

### Living Spaces: Bespoke Expression with Custom Stone Furniture

Bespoke stone furniture, such as coffee tables, sideboards, and shelving, brings artistry and individuality to living rooms and offices. StoneLab’s custom stone solutions ensure each piece harmonizes with the space, whether you seek Scandinavian minimalism or bold, tactile contrasts.

## Caring for Your Stone Surfaces for Lasting Luxury

To maintain the pristine appearance of exclusive stone interiors:

- **Wipe spills immediately** to prevent staining, especially on marble and limestone  
- **Use pH-neutral cleaners** designed for natural stone  
- **Apply sealant** as recommended to protect against moisture and etching  
- **Avoid harsh chemicals** and abrasive pads that can dull surfaces

Regular, gentle care will preserve the stone’s lustre for generations.

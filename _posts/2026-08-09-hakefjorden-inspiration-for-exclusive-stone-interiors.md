## Selecting the Perfect Stone for Every Space in Luxury Homes

Choosing the right stone is crucial to achieving harmony and longevity in your interior. Here are some expert tips:

- **Kitchens**: Opt for durable materials like granite or quartzite for kitchen stone countertops. These surfaces resist heat and staining, perfect for high-use areas.
- **Bathrooms**: Marble bathroom design remains a favorite for its luminous finish, but consider honed or matte surfaces to reduce slipperiness and maintenance.
- **Living areas**: Bespoke stone furniture, such as coffee tables or shelving, introduces tactile luxury and can be tailored to highlight unique veining or coloration.

## Caring for Your Stone Surfaces: Tips from StoneLab Experts

With the right care, your stone interiors will maintain their beauty for decades. StoneLab recommends:

- **Regular cleaning**: Use pH-neutral cleaners and soft cloths to avoid damage.
- **Sealants**: Periodically reseal porous stones like marble to protect against spills and staining.
- **Attention to detail**: Avoid harsh chemicals and abrasive pads that can scratch or dull the finish.

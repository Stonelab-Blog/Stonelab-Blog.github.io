## Selecting the Right Stone for Every Space

Choosing the perfect stone is both an art and a science. Here’s how to select the ideal material for your project:

- **Kitchens**: Opt for durable stones like granite or engineered quartz for countertops that withstand daily use while maintaining elegance.
- **Bathrooms**: Marble or limestone brings a spa-like tranquility, especially in larger formats for floors and walls.
- **Living Spaces**: Feature walls in textured stone or bespoke stone furniture create a captivating focal point.
- **Entryways**: Polished or honed stone tiles set a grand tone and offer excellent durability.

StoneLab’s experts guide you through selecting and customizing stones that reflect your aesthetic and lifestyle.

## Care and Maintenance for Lasting Beauty

Preserving the allure of your stone surfaces is simple with the right care:

- Wipe up spills promptly, especially on porous stones like marble.
- Use pH-neutral cleaners to avoid damaging the natural finish.
- Seal surfaces regularly, as recommended for your stone type.
- Avoid abrasive pads or harsh chemicals.

With thoughtful maintenance, your custom stone solutions will retain their brilliance for generations.

## Selecting the Right Stone for Every Space in Luxury Interiors

Choosing the ideal stone for each area of the home depends on both aesthetics and functionality. Here are some key considerations:

- **Kitchen Stone Countertops**: Opt for durable materials such as granite or quartzite that withstand daily use while offering sophisticated style.
- **Bathroom Retreats**: Marble bathroom design brings an air of spa-like luxury, with options in various hues and veining to suit your taste.
- **Living Spaces**: Limestone or travertine can provide a warm, inviting atmosphere, perfect for statement walls or bespoke stone furniture.
- **Entryways and Hallways**: Hardwearing surfaces like slate or basalt set a tone of refinement from the moment you step inside.

StoneLab’s expert team guides clients throughout Sweden, Scandinavia, Germany, and the UK through every stage, from stone selection and customization to final installation, ensuring every detail is tailored to perfection.

## Maintaining the Beauty of Your Stone Surfaces

To preserve the elegance and longevity of stone interiors, consider these care tips:

- **Routine Cleaning**: Use mild, pH-neutral cleaners specifically formulated for natural stone.
- **Sealant Application**: Regularly apply a quality sealant to protect against stains and moisture.
- **Prompt Spill Management**: Wipe up spills immediately, especially on porous surfaces like marble.
- **Avoid Harsh Chemicals**: Steer clear of acidic or abrasive products that can damage the stone’s finish.

With proper care, StoneLab’s custom stone solutions retain their pristine appearance for generations.

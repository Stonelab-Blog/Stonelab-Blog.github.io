## Selecting the Perfect Stone for Every Space

Choosing the right stone is both art and science. Consider these practical tips to ensure your next project is as functional as it is beautiful:

- **Kitchens**: For kitchen stone countertops, durability is key. Granite and quartzite offer resilience against heat and scratches, while marble provides unmatched elegance for statement islands.
- **Bathrooms**: Marble bathroom design remains a hallmark of luxury, but consider non-slip finishes for flooring and moisture-resistant stones for shower areas.
- **Living Spaces**: Bespoke stone furniture—coffee tables, sideboards, or shelving—can become sculptural focal points. Select stones with unique veining or coloration for added visual impact.
- **Entryways and Hallways**: High-traffic areas benefit from hardwearing options like granite or basalt, which retain their lustre with proper care.

## Maintaining the Beauty of Stone Surfaces

To keep your custom stone solutions looking their best for years to come, StoneLab recommends:

- **Regular Cleaning**: Use a gentle, pH-neutral cleaner and a soft cloth to prevent dulling or etching.
- **Sealing**: Natural stones such as marble and limestone should be sealed periodically to protect against staining.
- **Prompt Spill Management**: Wipe up spills immediately, particularly with acidic substances, to preserve the integrity of your surfaces.
- **Avoid Harsh Chemicals**: Steer clear of abrasive or acidic cleaners that can damage the stone’s finish.

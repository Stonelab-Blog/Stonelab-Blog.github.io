## Selecting the Right Stone for Each Space

Not all stones are created equal. The choice of material should consider both aesthetics and practical requirements:

- **Kitchen stone countertops:** Opt for hardwearing stones such as granite or quartzite for durability and easy maintenance.
- **Marble bathroom design:** Marble’s luxurious feel is perfect for bathrooms, but choose honed finishes and proper sealing for lasting beauty.
- **Living spaces:** Limestone and travertine add warmth and texture, while custom stone solutions can transform fireplaces or create unique shelving.

**Pro tip:** Always request samples and view them in your space’s natural light before finalizing your selection.

## Care and Maintenance for Lasting Beauty

To preserve the pristine appearance of exclusive stone interiors:

- **Wipe surfaces regularly** with a soft, damp cloth.
- **Use pH-neutral cleaners**—avoid acidic or abrasive agents.
- **Seal periodically** as recommended for the specific stone type.
- **Protect from heat and scratches** by using trivets and coasters on kitchen stone countertops and stone furniture.

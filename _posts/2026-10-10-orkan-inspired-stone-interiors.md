## Current Trends in Exclusive Stone Interiors

The orkan influence is reshaping how stone is used in luxury interiors:

- **Movement and Texture:** Veined marbles, textured granites, and layered onyx surfaces add depth and a sense of flowing energy.
- **Monolithic Statements:** Large-format stone installations—such as seamless stone kitchen islands or full-height bathroom walls—offer drama and sophistication.
- **Natural Tones:** Earthy hues, soft greys, and muted whites are favored for their calming effect, blending beautifully with Scandinavian and Northern European design sensibilities.
- **Functional Art:** Bespoke stone furniture like sculpted tables or integrated shelving brings artistry into functional spaces.

## Selecting the Right Stone for Each Space

Choosing the perfect stone depends on the room’s purpose and atmosphere:

- **Kitchen:** Opt for durable materials like granite or quartzite for kitchen stone countertops that can withstand high use and resist staining.
- **Bathroom:** Marble bathroom design remains a classic for its refined appearance, though sealed marble or porcelain alternatives are ideal for wet environments.
- **Living Spaces:** Limestone and travertine offer warmth and subtle sophistication for living rooms, foyers, and bespoke stone furniture.
- **Accent Features:** Onyx and rare marbles provide striking focal points for fireplaces or entryways.

## Stone Care and Maintenance Tips

Luxury materials deserve thoughtful care. Here’s how to preserve the beauty of your custom stone solutions:

- **Daily Cleaning:** Use a pH-neutral cleaner and a soft cloth to prevent surface damage.
- **Sealing:** Periodically seal stone surfaces, especially in kitchens and bathrooms, to protect against moisture and staining.
- **Avoid Abrasives:** Steer clear of harsh chemicals or abrasive pads that can scratch or dull the finish.
- **Prompt Spill Cleanup:** Quickly wipe away spills, particularly acidic substances like wine or citrus, to avoid etching.

## Selecting the Right Stone for Every Space

Every room has unique demands, and the right stone selection is crucial to achieving both aesthetics and functionality:

- **Kitchen:** Opt for durable granite or quartzite for countertops, combining resilience with elegance.
- **Bathroom:** Choose non-porous marble or limestone for vanities and flooring, balancing luxury with practicality.
- **Living Areas:** Employ statement stones such as onyx or travertine for fireplace surrounds or bespoke stone furniture, creating visual focal points.

StoneLab’s design consultants guide clients through each choice, ensuring every detail aligns with the project’s vision.

## Caring for Your Stone Surfaces

Maintaining the pristine appearance of your exclusive stone interiors is simple with a few essential practices:

- **Regular Cleaning:** Use a soft cloth and pH-neutral cleaner to preserve the natural finish.
- **Sealant Application:** Periodic sealing protects against stains and moisture, especially in high-traffic or wet areas.
- **Gentle Handling:** Avoid harsh chemicals and abrasive tools to prevent scratching and etching.

StoneLab provides tailored care recommendations for every bespoke installation, extending the life and beauty of your investment.

## Selecting the Right Stone for Every Space in Scandinavia and Northern Europe

Choosing the perfect stone is both an art and a science. Consider these guidelines when planning your next project:

- **Kitchen Countertops:** Opt for harder stones like granite or quartzite, which resist staining and scratching. Lighter hues can brighten up Scandinavian interiors, while darker tones add drama.
- **Bathrooms:** Marble remains a favorite for its calming elegance. Seal regularly to protect against water marks and maintain its luminous finish.
- **Living Spaces:** Limestone and travertine bring warmth and texture to floors and accent walls, blending beautifully with wood and metal.

## Care and Maintenance Tips for Lasting Beauty in Exclusive Stone Interiors

To keep your exclusive stone interiors pristine, follow these simple care routines:

- Wipe surfaces daily with a soft, damp cloth.
- Use pH-neutral cleaners specifically formulated for stone.
- Reseal countertops and high-traffic surfaces annually to prevent stains.
- Avoid harsh chemicals and acidic substances that can etch or discolor the stone.

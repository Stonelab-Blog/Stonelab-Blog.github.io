### Latest Trends in Exclusive Stone Interiors

Current interior trends, as seen in the most admired Hemnet listings, reveal a move towards bold, expressive stonework:

- **Contrasting Finishes:** Polished marble paired with honed granite creates a layered, tactile experience.
- **Bespoke Stone Furniture:** Custom dining tables, shelving, and accent pieces blur the line between art and utility.
- **Large Format Surfaces:** Expansive stone islands and floor-to-ceiling wall cladding bring drama and continuity to open-plan living spaces.
- **Sustainable Stone Sourcing:** Eco-conscious clients are choosing responsibly quarried stone, supporting both environmental stewardship and design integrity.

### Choosing the Perfect Stone for Every Space

Selecting the right stone for your home is both an art and a science. Here’s how to make the most of StoneLab’s custom stone solutions:

#### Kitchen

- **Countertops:** Opt for dense, non-porous stones such as quartzite or granite for longevity and easy maintenance.
- **Backsplashes:** Marble adds timeless elegance and pairs beautifully with modern cabinetry.

#### Bathroom

- **Vanities and Walls:** Marble bathroom design remains a top choice, offering luxury and tranquility.
- **Flooring:** Choose slip-resistant finishes for safety without compromising on style.

#### Living Areas

- **Fireplace Surrounds:** Dramatic stonework creates a captivating focal point.
- **Bespoke Stone Furniture:** Select statement pieces, such as coffee tables or benches, crafted to your specifications.

### Stone Care and Maintenance Tips

Preserving the beauty of your exclusive stone interiors is simple with a few thoughtful practices:

- **Seal Regularly:** Protect surfaces from stains and moisture by reapplying a high-quality sealant as recommended.
- **Gentle Cleaning:** Use pH-neutral cleaners and avoid harsh chemicals to maintain the stone’s natural luster.
- **Prevent Scratches:** Place pads under heavy objects and wipe up spills promptly to prevent etching or discoloration.

## Selecting the Right Stone for Every Space

Choosing the perfect stone begins with understanding the unique requirements of each area:

- **Kitchens:** Opt for hard-wearing, low-porosity stones such as granite or quartzite for countertops. These materials resist stains and scratches, making them ideal for culinary spaces.
- **Bathrooms:** Marble bathroom design remains a timeless choice, offering both luxury and practicality when properly sealed and maintained.
- **Living Areas:** For bespoke stone furniture and feature walls, consider stones with distinctive patterns or textures to create a focal point.
- **Entryways and Hallways:** Durable stones like terrazzo or limestone set a welcoming tone while withstanding high traffic.

## Stone Care and Maintenance Tips

To ensure your exclusive stone interiors retain their beauty for years to come, follow these essential tips:

- **Regular Cleaning:** Use a soft cloth and pH-neutral cleaner to prevent damage to the stone’s surface.
- **Prompt Spill Management:** Wipe up spills immediately—especially acidic substances like wine, citrus, or vinegar—to avoid staining.
- **Sealing:** Periodically seal natural stone surfaces to enhance resilience against moisture and wear.
- **Avoid Abrasives:** Steer clear of harsh chemicals and abrasive pads that can scratch or dull the finish.

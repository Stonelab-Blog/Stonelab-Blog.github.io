## Practical Advice for Selecting the Right Stone

Selecting the ideal stone for each space is a blend of aesthetic vision and practical consideration. Here are StoneLab’s expert tips:

### For Kitchens
- **Marble:** Exudes elegance but requires regular sealing; best for homeowners who appreciate natural patina.
- **Granite:** Highly durable and resistant to stains—ideal for busy family kitchens.
- **Quartzite:** Combines the look of marble with the resilience of granite.

### For Bathrooms
- **Soapstone:** Naturally non-porous and resistant to moisture, making it perfect for wet environments.
- **Limestone:** Soft, subtle beauty; works well in guest bathrooms with low traffic.

### For Living Spaces and Furniture
- **Onyx:** Striking veining and translucency, perfect for accent walls or lighting features.
- **Terrazzo:** Sustainable and customizable, ideal for bespoke stone furniture with a modern flair.

## Care and Maintenance Tips

Maintaining the beauty and longevity of exclusive stone interiors is simple with the right approach:
- **Clean regularly** with gentle, pH-neutral cleaners to prevent etching or staining.
- **Seal surfaces** annually or as recommended by your stone specialist, especially for marble and limestone.
- **Use coasters and trivets** to protect kitchen stone countertops from heat or acidic spills.
- **Avoid harsh chemicals** that can damage natural stone finishes.

StoneLab provides tailored care instructions for every custom project, ensuring your investment remains impeccable for years to come.

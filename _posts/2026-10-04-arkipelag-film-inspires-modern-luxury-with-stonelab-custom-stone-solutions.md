### How to Select the Perfect Stone for Each Space in Your Luxury Home or Project

Choosing the right stone is an art in itself. Here are key considerations for luxury homeowners, architects, and designers in Sweden, Scandinavia, and the UK:

#### Kitchens

- **Durability & Maintenance:** Opt for dense stones like granite or quartzite for kitchen stone countertops; they offer resistance to scratches and stains.
- **Visual Impact:** Marble, with its dramatic veining, elevates even minimalist kitchens into works of art.

#### Bathrooms

- **Elegance & Functionality:** Marble bathroom design remains a classic, but consider non-slip finishes for safety and comfort.
- **Moisture Resistance:** Select stones with low porosity for shower walls and floors.

#### Living Spaces & Furniture

- **Bespoke Stone Furniture:** Custom tables, fireplace surrounds, and shelving in natural stone create focal points that reflect personal style.
- **Harmony with Other Materials:** Pair stone with wood or metal for a balanced, layered look.

### Care and Maintenance Tips for Lasting Beauty of Your Custom Stone Interiors

To maintain the pristine elegance of your exclusive stone interiors:

- **Regular Cleaning:** Use gentle, pH-neutral cleaners to preserve the surface finish.
- **Seal Periodically:** Natural stones benefit from sealing to protect against stains and etching, especially in high-use areas.
- **Avoid Harsh Chemicals:** Steer clear of acidic or abrasive products that can damage the stone’s surface.

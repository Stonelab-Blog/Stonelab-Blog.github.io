### Expert Advice for Selecting the Perfect Stone for Your Luxury Interior Design

Choosing the right stone is essential to achieving a harmonious and long-lasting design. Consider these practical tips:

1. **Purpose and Placement**  
   Select materials that suit their intended use—granite or quartzite for high-traffic kitchens, marble for elegant bathroom retreats, and softer stones for decorative features.

2. **Color and Texture**  
   Harmonize stone tones with your overall palette. Lighter stones create an airy feel, while darker hues add drama and warmth.

3. **Sustainability in Stone Sourcing**  
   Opt for suppliers who prioritize sustainable stone sourcing. At StoneLab, we ensure that our luxury interior materials are responsibly quarried and processed with minimal environmental impact.

4. **Customization**  
   Embrace the bespoke. Whether you’re designing kitchen stone countertops or crafting a one-of-a-kind fireplace surround, StoneLab’s custom stone solutions offer endless possibilities tailored to your vision.

### Care and Maintenance for Lasting Beauty of Exclusive Stone Interiors

Preserving the allure of exclusive stone interiors requires thoughtful care:

- **Seal Regularly**: Protect porous surfaces like marble and limestone with a high-quality sealant, especially in bathrooms and kitchens.
- **Gentle Cleaning**: Use pH-neutral cleaners and soft cloths to prevent etching and staining.
- **Preventative Measures**: Place mats or coasters under glasses and hot cookware to avoid surface damage.
- **Prompt Attention**: Wipe spills immediately, particularly from acidic substances such as citrus and wine.

## Selecting the Perfect Stone for Each Space

Choosing the right stone is essential for achieving both beauty and practicality. Here’s how to make the best choice for your interior:

### Kitchens

Opt for hard-wearing stones like granite or quartzite for countertops. Their resistance to stains and scratches makes them ideal for culinary spaces, while their rich colors elevate the overall design.

### Bathrooms

Marble remains the timeless choice for luxury bathrooms. Its cool touch and subtle veining evoke a spa-like atmosphere, perfect for creating a private sanctuary.

### Living Spaces

For bespoke stone furniture and feature walls, consider travertine or limestone. These materials offer warmth and texture, complementing both modern and classic interiors.

## Caring for Your Stone Surfaces

To preserve the beauty of your custom stone solutions:

- **Clean gently:** Use a pH-neutral cleaner and avoid abrasive pads.
- **Seal regularly:** Especially for marble and limestone, to protect against moisture and stains.
- **Avoid harsh chemicals:** They can dull the surface and damage the finish.

With proper care, your stone interiors will continue to shine, season after season.

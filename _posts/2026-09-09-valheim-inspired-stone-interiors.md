### Selecting the Right Stone for Every Space

Choosing the ideal stone is essential for both function and aesthetics. Here’s what to consider:

- **Kitchens:** For stone countertops, opt for quartzite or granite for superior durability and stain resistance. These materials provide a practical yet luxurious surface for daily use.
- **Bathrooms:** Marble is a classic choice for bathroom design, creating a spa-like atmosphere. Ensure the stone is properly sealed to maintain its beauty over time.
- **Furniture and accents:** Bespoke stone furniture pieces, such as coffee tables or shelving, can serve as both functional elements and works of art. Select stones with striking veining or unique coloration for maximum impact.

### Caring for Your Stone Surfaces

To preserve the beauty of your custom stone solutions:

- **Clean gently:** Use pH-neutral cleaners and avoid harsh chemicals that may damage the surface.
- **Seal regularly:** Especially important for marble and other porous stones, sealing protects against stains and etching.
- **Wipe spills promptly:** Particularly with acidic substances, which can dull the finish of natural stone.

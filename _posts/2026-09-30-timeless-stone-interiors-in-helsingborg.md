## Choosing the Right Stone for Every Space

Every room has its own requirements and ambiance, and selecting the ideal stone is essential for both beauty and practicality. Here’s how to approach the decision:

- **Kitchens**: Opt for durable, stain-resistant options like quartzite or granite for kitchen stone countertops. These materials withstand daily use while maintaining their luxurious sheen.
- **Bathrooms**: Marble remains the gold standard for bathroom retreats, offering a spa-like atmosphere. Ensure it’s properly sealed to prevent moisture absorption.
- **Living Rooms**: For statement pieces, consider bespoke stone furniture in limestone or travertine, which lend a sculptural presence without overwhelming the space.
- **Accent Features**: Slate and soapstone are excellent for fireplace surrounds or feature walls, adding texture and depth.

## Care and Maintenance for Lasting Beauty

Stone surfaces offer exceptional longevity, but a few thoughtful practices will preserve their brilliance:

- **Regular Cleaning**: Use pH-neutral cleaners specifically formulated for stone. Avoid harsh chemicals that may damage the surface.
- **Sealing**: Apply a quality sealant to porous stones like marble and limestone to prevent staining.
- **Immediate Attention to Spills**: Especially with natural stones, blot spills promptly to avoid etching or discoloration.
- **Gentle Materials**: Use soft cloths and non-abrasive sponges to protect the finish.

## Current Trends in Interior Stone Design

The Bauhaus norge aesthetic champions minimalism, geometric shapes, and a harmonious blend of materials. In today’s luxury interiors, this translates into:

- **Monolithic Kitchen Islands:** Sleek kitchen stone countertops in neutral palettes such as Nordic marble or deep granite.
- **Integrated Stone Furniture:** Custom stone tables or shelving units that function as sculptural centerpieces.
- **Spa-Like Bathrooms:** Marble bathroom design with large-format tiles, seamless vanities, and subtle veining for understated opulence.
- **Natural Textures:** Honed and matte finishes that highlight the innate beauty of stone, echoing Bauhaus principles of form and function.

## Selecting the Right Stone for Every Space

Choosing the perfect stone for your interior requires a thoughtful approach:

1. **Consider Functionality:** For high-traffic areas like kitchens, opt for durable, stain-resistant materials such as granite or engineered stone.
2. **Prioritize Aesthetics:** Marble and limestone offer luxurious visual impact, ideal for bathrooms or statement walls.
3. **Match Stone to Light:** Lighter stones enhance natural light in Scandinavian interiors, while darker stones bring drama and warmth.
4. **Customization Options:** Collaborate with StoneLab’s experts to design bespoke stone furniture or unique finishes that reflect your personal style.

## Maintaining the Beauty of Stone Surfaces

StoneLab’s exclusive stone interiors are crafted to endure. However, proper care ensures they retain their allure:

- Use pH-neutral cleansers to protect finishes.
- Quickly wipe spills to prevent staining, especially on marble surfaces.
- Seal porous stones periodically for enhanced resistance.
- Avoid abrasive pads; opt for soft cloths to preserve the natural sheen.

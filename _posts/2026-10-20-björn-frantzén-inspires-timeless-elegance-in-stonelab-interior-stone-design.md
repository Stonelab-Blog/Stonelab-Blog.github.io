### Selecting the Right Stone for Every Space in Your Luxury Interior

Choosing the perfect stone requires balancing aesthetics, practicality, and sustainability. Here are a few expert tips from StoneLab, specialists in exclusive stone interiors:

#### For Kitchens in Germany and the UK
- **Quartzite or Granite:** Ideal for kitchen stone countertops thanks to their resilience and stain resistance.
- **Marble:** A timeless choice for islands or feature walls, offering unrivaled elegance with proper care.

#### For Bathrooms
- **Marble:** Its natural beauty and light-reflecting qualities create a sense of sanctuary in marble bathroom designs.
- **Limestone or Travertine:** Soft to the touch, these stones are best for low-traffic areas or decorative touches.

#### For Living Spaces and Bespoke Stone Furniture
- **Bespoke Stone Furniture:** Statement pieces like coffee tables or shelving in rare stones add exclusivity and visual interest.
- **Accent Walls:** StoneLab crafts unique feature walls that anchor luxury interiors with texture and depth.

### Care and Maintenance Tips for Your Custom Stone Solutions

To preserve the allure of your custom stone solutions:

- **Use pH-neutral cleaners** and avoid acidic substances, especially on marble surfaces.
- **Seal stone surfaces** regularly to enhance durability and prevent stains.
- **Wipe spills promptly** to maintain the pristine look of exclusive stone interiors.
- **Avoid heavy impact** on edges and corners of stone furniture to prevent chipping.

StoneLab provides tailored care advice for each installation, ensuring every surface stands the test of time.

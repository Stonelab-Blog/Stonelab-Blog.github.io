## Choosing the Right Stone for Every Space

Selecting the ideal stone for your interior requires careful consideration of both form and function. Here are some practical tips from the experts at StoneLab:

- **Kitchens:** Opt for dense, low-porosity stones such as granite or quartzite for kitchen stone countertops. These materials resist staining and withstand heavy use.
- **Bathrooms:** Marble remains a classic for its luxurious look, though sealed limestone or engineered stones can offer similar elegance with enhanced durability.
- **Living and Dining Areas:** Consider bespoke stone furniture or feature walls in travertine, slate, or textured granite to add tactile richness.
- **Accent Pieces:** For exclusive stone interiors, unique stone accents can become focal points—think carved mantels or custom coffee tables.

## Care and Maintenance Tips for Lasting Beauty

Preserving the natural allure of your stone surfaces is simple with the right approach:

- **Clean Regularly:** Use a soft cloth and pH-neutral cleanser to avoid damaging the stone’s surface.
- **Seal Periodically:** Proper sealing protects against stains and enhances the stone’s natural sheen.
- **Avoid Abrasive Tools:** Steer clear of harsh scrubbers or acidic cleaners, which can etch or dull the finish.
- **Address Spills Promptly:** Especially on marble and limestone, clean spills immediately to prevent staining.

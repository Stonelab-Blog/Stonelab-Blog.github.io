### Practical Advice for Selecting the Right Stone

Choosing the ideal stone for your space requires careful consideration of both aesthetics and usage. Here are some expert tips:

- **Functionality first:** For high-traffic areas like kitchens, opt for durable materials such as granite or quartzite, which withstand daily wear and resist staining.
- **Atmosphere matters:** Soft marbles or limestones create a serene, luxurious feel in bathrooms and bedrooms, while bold stones add drama to entryways or living spaces.
- **Color and veining:** Consider your existing palette and the mood you wish to evoke—subtle veining offers quiet sophistication, while vibrant patterns make a statement.

Our StoneLab consultants can guide you through every step, ensuring your custom stone solutions reflect your vision and lifestyle.

### Caring for and Maintaining Stone Surfaces

To preserve the beauty and value of exclusive stone interiors, consistent care is essential:

- **Seal regularly:** Most stones benefit from periodic sealing to protect against spills and moisture.
- **Clean gently:** Use pH-neutral cleaners and soft cloths to avoid etching or scratching surfaces.
- **Address spills quickly:** Especially with porous stones, prompt attention prevents staining.

StoneLab provides tailored care advice for each material, ensuring your investment remains impeccable for generations.

### Selecting the Perfect Stone for Every Space in Scandinavia and Germany

Choosing the right material is essential to achieving both durability and elegance. Here are our recommendations for key living areas popular in the Scandinavian and German markets:

- **Kitchens:** Opt for granite or quartzite for kitchen stone countertops—these stones offer exceptional resilience and timeless appeal.
- **Bathrooms:** Marble is ideal for bathroom surfaces, bringing a spa-like tranquility and effortless sophistication.
- **Living Areas:** Limestone and travertine are excellent for flooring, offering warmth and unique patterning.
- **Furniture:** For bespoke stone furniture, consider a statement material like onyx for visual impact.

We recommend working with a specialist to ensure the selected stone complements your interior design and lifestyle needs.

### Care and Maintenance Tips for Lasting Beauty of Luxury Interior Materials

To preserve the lustre and integrity of your stone surfaces:

- Clean regularly with a pH-neutral cleaner.
- Use coasters and mats to prevent scratches or stains.
- Seal porous stones periodically to enhance protection.
- Avoid harsh chemicals that may erode or dull the finish.

With proper care, your custom stone features will retain their elegance for generations.

### Selecting the Right Stone for Every Space

Choosing the ideal stone is both an art and a science. Here are refined tips for selecting the perfect material:

- **Kitchens:** Opt for durable stones like granite or quartzite for countertops—offering resistance to heat and scratches while maintaining luxury appeal.
- **Bathrooms:** Marble bathroom design remains a classic, providing an elegant, spa-like atmosphere. For floors and walls, consider slip-resistant finishes.
- **Living Spaces:** Bespoke stone furniture or feature walls in limestone or travertine add a subtle yet striking focal point.
- **Outdoor Areas:** Weather-resistant materials such as slate or granite are ideal for terraces and garden features, blending seamlessly with Scandinavian landscapes.

### Care and Maintenance for Lasting Beauty

StoneLab’s exclusive stone interiors are designed for longevity. Maintain their brilliance with these simple guidelines:

- **Regular Cleaning:** Use pH-neutral cleaners and soft cloths to protect the stone’s natural finish.
- **Sealant Application:** Periodically reseal surfaces to guard against stains and moisture.
- **Avoid Abrasives:** Steer clear of harsh chemicals or abrasive pads, especially on polished and delicate stones.
- **Quick Response:** Wipe spills immediately, particularly acidic substances like wine or citrus, to prevent etching.
